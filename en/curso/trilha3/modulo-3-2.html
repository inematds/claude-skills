🧩 The suite and the orchestrator

Funnel Builder isn’t just one skill — it’s a suite of specialist skills, each handling one piece: landing page generator, email generator, lead magnet creator, VSL script generator, webinar script generator, quiz flow creator, social content pack, thank-you page creator, upsell creator, headline/hook generator, affiliate kit generator, challenge content generator. At the center of it all, a orchestrator decides who gets in and in what order.

Anyone who builds funnels knows: what always caused delays wasn’t thinking through the strategy—it was write every page, every email, every post, keep everything consistent, and see it through. The suite tackles exactly this execution bottleneck.

🚦 How to start a project

Getting started matters as much as the skill. Follow this routine and the results improve dramatically. These are seven short steps that give the suite the right context before it starts working.

1

A new session for each funnel

Don’t continue an old conversation. The suite isolates each project so nothing leaks from previous work — without this, it might offer to reuse an email from another project.

2

Describe the offer in one sentence

Who it's for, what they get, how much it costs. "A $27 mini-course for freelancers who want to raise their rates without losing clients" outperforms "a course about pricing".

3

Paste the existing assets

Have a draft sales page? A course outline? A bio? Paste everything in before starting — it uses your actual content instead of generic placeholders.

4

Specify the platform and type + goal

Kajabi, ClickFunnels, Webflow, Squarespace, plain HTML. If you don’t specify, it uses clean HTML with notes. Also mention the funnel type and goal (build a list? sell? book appointments?) so it can choose the map.

✓ Good start

  • ✓"Start fresh, don't refer to anything from before."
  • ✓A clear offer in one sentence, with a price.
  • ✓Platform and funnel type named.
  • ✓Request the magnet as a downloadable file.

✗ Bad start

  • ✗Continue an old chat (context leaks).
  • ✗Vague offer ("a course about X").
  • ✗Don’t state the platform or type.
  • ✗Forget to paste in the assets that already exist.

📋 Intake, plan, and "build it"

After kickoff, the suite guides you through a intake: asks a few questions (email platform, whether you want a thank-you page, etc.) and builds a complete asset map — every deliverable, in order. You review this plan and give one approval: "build it". From there, it doesn't pause to ask permission for every piece — it goes all the way, saving and confirming each asset.

🎬 The funnel in practice: the webinar

To wrap up, a real case. A short brief—a webinar for a trading journal called "Options Vault", at a specific date and time, live, about the importance of keeping a journal, with a free account for anyone who registers—becomes an entire funnel. The suite still reads the brand site to capture the style and branding before building.

1

Webinar script (~75 min)

From the pre-show ("where are you watching from today?") to the credibility section, content pillars, transition to the offer, Q&A, and closing. With slide notes ("centered logo on a navy background, timer in the corner").

2

Registration & confirmation pages

Copy and design tailored to the topic, with “this webinar is for you if...” and “it’s not for you if...,” “save my spot” buttons, and confirmation with an add-to-calendar option and a prompt to activate a free account.

3

Three email sequences

Reminders (immediate confirmation, 24 hours before, 1 hour before, “we’re live”), emails for those who watched (replay + next step), and a no-show follow-up for those who missed it.

4

Replay page & social pack

A replay page ready to go—just add the video and date—and a social pack of 7 posts (LinkedIn, Twitter) with different angles. All in one folder, with a walkthrough at the end.

📊 The same engine, different funnels

  • •Lead generation (pool company): “crystal-clear pool” checklist → index page that addresses the pain point, landing page, thank-you page, PDF with target ranges, and emails.
  • •Lead magnet (mindfulness): opt-in, thank-you page that sells a $7 journal, delivery and check-in emails, and the journal PDF—all from a minimal prompt.

🛠️ Practical exercises

Exercise 1 — Choose the right type

Choose 3 of your offers (or make some up) and, for each one, decide which of the 11 funnel types fits best and why. List the first asset the map would require in each case. This trains you to read dependencies.

Exercise 2 — Write the offer in one sentence

Turn a vague offer into a clear sentence in the “who · what · how much” format. Use this template:

One [format: mini-course / ebook / service] of [price] for [specific audience] that want [result] without [pain avoided].

Exercise 3 — Create a runnable SKILL.md (minimal orchestrator)

Write a lean funnel orchestrator. Save it as funnel-builder/SKILL.md in your project's skills folder and launch it with an offer brief. It should do intake, show the asset map, and wait for your "build it".
